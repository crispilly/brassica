Brassica is a free and open source recipe manager for Android.

Recipes are stored locally on the device and can include images, ingredients, directions, notes, nutrition information and categories.

Features:
• Create, edit and search recipes and mark favorites
• Filter by multiple categories using OR or AND matching
• Seasonal calendar and seasonal recipe filtering
• Cooking mode with an adapted presentation
• Import and export .broccoli and .broccoli-archive files
• Share individual recipes as files
• Import public recipe shares using QR codes or links
• Optional bidirectional synchronization with a Brassica Web installation

The Android app works fully offline without an account or server. Synchronization with Brassica Web is optional.

Brassica is a fork of the free Broccoli app and is licensed under GNU GPL v3.
